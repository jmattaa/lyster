# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with static output. Chosen by the user so the frontend can ship as a standalone static site now and later be served by, or paired with, a Go backend.

## Users

A broad Swedish restaurant audience, planning mostly on a phone. Weekday lunch guests deciding quickly; evening and weekend guests looking for a place worth booking ahead. They want to know what kind of restaurant this is, see the food, check opening hours and location, and book a table without friction.

## Product Purpose

The public website for Lyster, a restaurant serving modern husmanskost: Swedish classics with a wider European table. It exists to turn curiosity into a booked table and to make the menu, the room, and the restaurant's point of view legible at a glance. Success means a visitor understands the concept within seconds, opens the menu, and either books or saves the address.

## Positioning

Modern husmanskost, defined plainly: Swedish husmanskost is the foundation, and European everyday dishes — pasta, schnitzel, moules-frites — sit beside it with the same standards. Not fusion for its own sake, and not a museum of Swedish food. Classic dishes are kept recognisable but rebuilt with better technique, seasonal produce, and precise sourcing. The restaurant is warm and generous rather than solemn, and the website should make familiar food feel newly worth crossing town for.

## Operating Context

Guests arrive from search, Instagram, or word of mouth. The common path is: what is this place, what does it cost, when is it open, where is it, can I book. Lunch and dinner are both part of the offer. The site must work as a standalone static site with no backend dependency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Landing page plus a separate menu page with shared navigation.
- Swedish only.
- External booking link; a clearly marked placeholder until the real booking URL exists.
- No real backend assumed. No forms that pretend to submit.
- Static output deployable to any static host.
- Contact details, opening hours, prices, and booking URL are sample values and must be marked for replacement.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Lyster.
- Concept: modern husmanskost — Swedish husmanskost plus other European dishes, such as pasta.
- Voice: warm, grounded, generous. Short sentences, no clichés, no luxury bluster.
- Palette is fixed: beige, blue, and gold.

## Evidence on Hand

No real photography, menu, prices, address, phone number, opening hours, booking URL, or staff information has been supplied. All such content is sample material authored for the design and must be replaced before launch. No testimonials, press quotes, awards, or customer claims may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Familiar food, treated with seriousness and warmth — Swedish first, European alongside.
2. The menu is the proof; show it plainly and make it easy to read.
3. Booking is never more than one clear action away.
4. Speak like a host, not a marketing department.
5. Work beautifully on a phone, first and always.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was established beyond normal web expectations. The build should meet WCAG 2.2 AA in contrast, focus states, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion behaviour.
